About Palawan Homes

Your trusted partner for renting in the Philippines — a fictional demo marketplace.

Find rental properties across the Philippines

Palawan Homes is a sample rental marketplace built for demonstration. It brings together apartments, condos, houses, studios and commercial spaces across major cities like Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo and Baguio. Filter by location, budget, bedrooms and property type to quickly shortlist the homes that fit your lifestyle.

Why people choose Palawan Homes

Every listing here is fictional, but the experience mirrors a modern marketplace: verified badges on featured homes, direct messaging with rent managers, video-style property reels and neighborhood guides — all designed to make renting simpler, more transparent and stress-free.
